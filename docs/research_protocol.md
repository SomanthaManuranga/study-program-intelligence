# Research protocol

Status: draft v1, 14 Oct 2026

## Primary question

Which new or substantially differentiated degree programme should OTH Amberg-Weiden consider, given new student demand, differentiation from existing programmes, graduate outlet and faculty feasibility?

## Scope (to confirm with supervisor)

- Faculty: Electrical Engineering, Media and Computer Science
- Degree level: both master and bachelor
- Geography: within whole germany.

## Four claims

- A. Demand: enough prospective students want this field, after demographic change is removed.
- B. Capture: OTH can win a realistic share, given competitors and location.
- C. Fit: the faculty can teach it with existing staff, labs and modules, or with a costed gap.
- D. Outlet: graduates find relevant jobs, especially in the region.

A candidate wins only if all four hold and the feasibility gate passes.

## Incrementality

Students who would otherwise study another OTH programme are not new enrolment for OTH. Report total and new-to-OTH enrolment separately.

## Known conflicts of interest

One team member is enrolled in the Master Artificial Intelligence for Industrial Applications at OTH. This is declared here, and every AI-related score is checked by the other team member.
