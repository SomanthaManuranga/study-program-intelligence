# Research protocol

Status: draft v2, 14 Oct 2026 (scope confirmed)

## Project brief (as given by the course)

Use public data (Destatis, other universities' websites, ...) to find and argue which new study programme would
attract the most students and why it suits our faculty. (Recorded 18 Oct 2026 from the course brief.)

## Primary question

Which new or substantially differentiated degree programme should OTH Amberg-Weiden consider, at bachelor or master level, given new student demand, differentiation from existing programmes, graduate outlet and feasibility for the Faculty of Elektrotechnik, Medien und Informatik (EMI)?

## Scope (confirmed 14 Oct 2026)

- Faculty: Elektrotechnik, Medien und Informatik (EMI), as set out in the project brief.
- Degree levels: bachelor and master, analysed separately and then compared.
- Geography: all of Germany for demand and competition. Capture is estimated separately, using the Amberg-Weiden region (catchment within 100 and 150 km) for bachelor students and a wider national and international view for master students.
- Data: publicly available sources only. No primary survey. No internal OTH data.

## Four claims

- A. Demand: enough prospective students across Germany want this field, after demographic change is removed.
- B. Capture: OTH can win a realistic share, given competitors and location. Estimated from public data only.
- C. Fit: EMI can teach it with existing staff, labs and modules, or with a costed gap.
- D. Outlet: graduates find relevant jobs, in Germany and in the region.

A candidate wins only if all four hold and the feasibility gate passes.

## Two levels, linked demand

Bachelor demand is measured by first-year entrants across Germany and the school-leaver pool in the catchment.

Master demand is measured by entrants into master programmes nationally, and its pipeline is the number of bachelor graduates in related fields across Germany, plus international applicants. Verify the GENESIS dimensions for master entrants before using them.

Both levels use the same criteria, but their demand indicators differ. A new master is partly fed by OTH's own bachelor graduates, so its incrementality must be checked against them.

## Incrementality

Students who would otherwise study another OTH programme are not new enrolment for OTH. Report total and new-to-OTH enrolment separately.

## Known limitation

Capture and fit rely on public data only: programme descriptions, module handbooks, staff pages and published statistics. The team has no OTH application or admission figures, so capture is estimated as a range and stated as an assumption in the report.

## Known conflicts of interest

One team member is enrolled in the Master Artificial Intelligence for Industrial Applications at OTH. This is declared here. Every AI-related score is checked by the other team member.
