Status: collected 31 Oct 2026 by the external reviewer (search-engine check, not a catalogue harvest); **pending
Soma's review**. Scope per the re-scope plan: a light check of same-type programmes in the Czech Republic (Plzeň

## Question

Could a neighbouring-country programme change robotics path B's differentiation score (central 3: "5 dedicated
institutions within 150 km, none within 80 km") or the competitive reading?

## What was checked, and what was found

- **Reachable foreign sites.** Within 150 km of Amberg the only university city across the border is Plzeň
  (Pilsen), about 115 km straight line (the distance already stated in docs/evidence_candidates.md). Austria's
  nearest university sites (Linz / Upper Austria, about 215 km; Hagenberg farther) lie outside the 150-km radius,
  so Austria cannot move a 150-km count for any candidate.
- **Plzeň: no robotics-titled bachelor found.** The University of West Bohemia (ZČU), Faculty of Applied Sciences
  (FAV), offers the bachelor "Kybernetika a řídicí technika" (code B0714A150005) with the specialisations
  "Automatické řízení a robotika" (automatic control and robotics) and "Umělá inteligence a automatizace"
  (programme structure in a ZČU document; the programme also appears on the official study portal studyin.gov.cz,
  which lists the follow-on master of the same name). No bachelor with robotics in the TITLE was found at ZČU;
  robotics appears as a specialisation inside the cybernetics programme. ZČU admission brochures (Gaudeamus)
  list the programme with double-digit to low-triple-digit applicant numbers (figures not verified in an official
  statistic).
- **Sources (retrieved 31 Oct 2026):** studyin.gov.cz programme page "Kybernetika a řídicí technika"

## Reading under the pre-registered rule

- The differentiation rule counts **dedicated (title-matched) institutions** within 150 km; focus-area programmes
  enter the capture model at weight w, not the differentiation count. ZČU's cybernetics bachelor is a
  **focus-type** competitor for robotics, not a dedicated one, so **robotics path B's differentiation score (3) does
  not change under the rule as written.**
- The German-only catalogue is itself pre-registered scope (competition is measured Germany-wide); counting foreign
  sites would be a method change and belongs to a logged sensitivity, not a score edit.
- Competitive substance: a Czech-taught programme competes only weakly for German-speaking school leavers (language
  barrier, different admission system), which the catchment model does not represent; the main effect would run the
  other way (Czech students crossing to OTH), which the capture model also does not count. Both effects are small
  against the anchor uncertainty.

## What would change this reading

A robotics-TITLED full-time bachelor within 150 km (none found in Plzeň; Austria out of range), or a decision by
Soma to extend the competition universe across the border (then ZČU enters as one focus-type site in a sensitivity
run). If wanted, the clean follow-up is a half-day harvest of the official Czech register (studyin.gov.cz or the
MŠMT accreditation register) for the shortlist themes, with robots.txt checked as usual.
