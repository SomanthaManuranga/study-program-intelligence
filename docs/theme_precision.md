# Theme rules: hand-labelled precision check (Step 38)

Status: 17 Oct 2026. Tables: `reports/theme_check.md`, generated by
`python -m study_program_intelligence.analysis.theme_check score`. Method: docs/decision_log.md (2026-10-17, two entries).

## Method

- Rules: the title keyword rules in `analysis/themes.py`, applied to titles as in the supply counts
  (`analysis/first_look.py`). Universe: 4,806 bachelor and master offers in data/processed/programmes.csv
  (BA Studiensuche / Hochschulkompass, 14 Oct 2026).
- Sheet: `analysis/theme_check.py sample` (seed 20261017), 110 rows: all 70 cybersecurity title matches,
  random samples of 10 robotics, 10 ai_data and 5 embedded_iot title matches, and a miss check of 15 programmes
  (5 per theme) whose title matches none of cybersecurity/robotics/ai_data but whose content text matches one.
- Question per row: is this a specialised programme of the theme (main focus, or a named track in the title)?
- **Labelling: labels were proposed by Claude (AI), with an English summary of each German content text, and
  each of the 110 labels was reviewed by a team member (Somantha Manuranga), who agreed with all 110.**
  Labelled file: docs/theme_labels.csv (committed; originally data/interim/theme_labels_EN.xlsx), joined on row_id
  (titles checked to match).
- Precision intervals are 95 % Wilson intervals; samples of 5-10 give wide intervals.

## What this means for the argument

- Claim B (capture): the cyber supply count is now exact. 31 dedicated bachelor and 32 master programmes in Germany,
  4 and 6 within 150 km, none at OTH. The earlier "4 BA / 7 MA within 150 km" had one false match (MBA Defence & Security).
- Competition is larger than the dedicated count suggests: 126 general programmes in Germany offer cybersecurity as
  a focus area, 18 of them within 150 km (16 outside OTH, incl. both Regensburg institutions at 51 km).
- Incrementality: a new OTH cyber bachelor would compete with OTH's own Informationssicherheit focus in
  Medieninformatik and Industrie-4.0-Informatik. This must be covered in the cannibalisation test and kill rule 1
  (docs/overlap_test.md) before the shortlist is final.
- Caveat: focus areas come from a content teaser (max ~400 characters, 49 of 2,901 lists cut off), so secondary
  counts are lower bounds.
