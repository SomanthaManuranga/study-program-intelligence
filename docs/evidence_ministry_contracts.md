**TH Ingolstadt** (one passage only): "... hat aber in den Berufungsgebieten der Informatik, speziell im Bereich KI und
Cybersicherheit, massive Probleme weibliche Bewerbungen zu generieren." (PDF p. 12). THI recruits professors in AI and
cybersecurity and finds it hard to attract women applicants. It says nothing about programmes or capacity.

**Reading:**

- Neither the OTH nor the Deggendorf contract names a planned full-time degree programme, a capacity or an intake.
- OTH's contract names IT security as a cross-cutting research field (supports fit). All other mentions of IT
  security concern the universities' own IT operations.
- TH Deggendorf's security transfer centre makes it a stronger regional competitor in security than the module
  comparison alone shows.

## Use in the argument

- **Claim C (fit):** OTH's contract names IT security ("IT-Security") as a cross-cutting research profile field next to
  digitalisation and AI. This supports institutional fit; it does not change the fit score, which rests on the module
  comparison.
- **Claim B (competition):** TH Deggendorf runs the Bavarian centre for digital security (technology transfer centre
  Vilshofen) and is system-accredited. It is a stronger regional competitor in security than the module comparison
  alone shows; this supports keeping the differentiation score at 3 and the advice that OT is no differentiator against
  TH Deggendorf.
- **Capacity and intake:** none of the three contracts names a planned degree programme, a capacity or an intake for
  cybersecurity.
