# Evidence: demand vs. competition (first look)

Status: v1, 14 Oct 2026. Tables: `reports/first_look.md`, generated by
`python -m study_program_intelligence.analysis.first_look`. All growth rates are own calculations.

## Summary for the decision

1. **Computer science is the only large EMI field still growing.** First-year students in Informatik rose from 18,586 (2019) to 21,428 (2025) in Germany while all subjects fell 1.0 % a year; in Bavaria they rose 6.1 % a year (2019–2024). Nationally the number has been flat since 2023 (21,434 → 22,083 → 21,428).
2. **The electrical and media side is shrinking.** Elektrotechnik −2.6 %/yr (Germany 2019–25), Mechatronik −3.6 %/yr, Medieninformatik −3.5 %/yr, Medientechnik −8.6 %/yr; in Bavaria Medientechnik fell 13 %/yr and Medieninformatik 8 %/yr (2019–24).
3. **A new generic AI programme is not a gap.** 122 bachelor and 152 master programmes in Germany have AI or data in the title; 32 and 34 of them lie within 150 km of OTH, and OTH already offers both levels.
4. **Cybersecurity is the clearest supply gap inside the growing field.** 34 bachelor and 36 master programmes in Germany, only 4 and 7 within 150 km, none at OTH; the nearest are FAU (63 km), TH Ingolstadt (82 km) and TH Deggendorf (105 km). 79 % of bachelor offers are at HAW, so it fits OTH's type.
5. **Robotics and embedded/IoT are thin on the ground but sit on shrinking feeder subjects.** Embedded/IoT has 5 titled bachelor programmes in Germany and none within 150 km; robotics has 1 bachelor offer within 100 km. Their natural feeders (Mechatronik, Technische Informatik) are flat or falling, so they need a combined angle (e.g. AI + robotics, secure embedded systems) to ride computer-science demand.

**Provisional shortlist for full dossiers** (to be tested against the kill rules and feasibility):
- Cybersecurity / secure systems: bachelor, and a master variant (industrial/OT security fits EMI's automation profile).
- Embedded / cyber-physical systems with AI or security focus: master first.
- Robotics and autonomous systems, combined with AI: bachelor.
- Control case: a differentiated AI variant (part-time master for professionals), to test the incrementality rule.

## Caveats

- Theme rules are keyword rules on titles; precision checked by hand for cybersecurity, robotics and embedded (one false positive fixed: "cyber-physical"). A labelled sample of 50 programmes is still to do.
- Programme counts measure supply, not enrolment or capacity. Several entries can be variants of one programme (full-time, dual, part-time): 4,840 offers vs. 4,756 unique title-institution pairs.
- Catalogue coverage: some OTH programmes known from other sources (e.g. the master Artificial Intelligence for Industrial Applications, the master Motorsport Engineering) do not appear in these 13 fields. Coverage must be checked before the overlap test.
- GENESIS counts winter-semester entrants only; summer starts are missing.
- Content texts in the catalogue are short (about 200 characters on average); the NLP gap analysis needs module handbooks for the shortlist.
