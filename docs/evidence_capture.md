# Evidence: capture model for an OTH Cyber Security B.Sc. (Claim B)

Status: v1, 17 Oct 2026 (Step 47). Tables: `reports/capture.md`, generated by
`python -m study_program_intelligence.analysis.capture`. Method decisions: docs/decision_log.md (Step 47).

## Note on the Step 46 forecast used here

The "central" demand value from Step 46 (e.g. Bavaria cyber proxy ~5,540 first-years in 2030) is **a midpoint
between the KMK floor (constant subject share) and the drift trend, chosen by us as a planning value; it is not a
model output.** The capture model below works with the observed 2023/24 pool; the Step 42/46 paths change that pool
by only a few per cent to 2030, which is small against the uncertainty of the intake anchors.

## Model

1. **Distance decay (calibrated):** doubly-constrained gravity model of Tabelle 5 (flows from 7 Bezirke to 17 state
   HAWs, population-weighted Bezirk centres, campus coordinates). Best beta = 1.6 (SRMSE 0.50, R2 0.95 of the flows;
   beta within 10 % of the best fit: 1.3-1.95). The model under-predicts home-region shares (OTH: model 61 % vs actual
   71 %; THI 37 % vs 71 %), partly because Bezirke are coarse; real local pull is stronger.
2. **Cyber demand per county:** qualified school leavers 2023/24 (Step 42) x Bavarian cyber-proxy first-year rate
   (4,936 / 54,698 = 9.0 %) x dedicated-cyber share s (3.9 % supply share of dedicated cyber bachelors among Bavarian
   informatics-type bachelors; 12.6 % search-interest share, Step 37).
3. **Huff split** of each county's demand among the full-time dedicated cyber bachelors (new OTH, THI, TH Deggendorf,
   THWS, HNU), plus, as sensitivity, Bavarian general bachelors with a security focus area (Step 38) at weight
   w = 0 / 0.5 / 1.

## How well the model reproduces reality

- Spatial pattern: good at Bezirk level (R2 0.95), but home shares are too low (see above).
- **Absolute scale: fails.** The uncalibrated demand model implies 59-173 first-years for THI's cyber bachelor
  against a planned capacity of 25 with no admission limit. The demand side (rate x share) is therefore not credible
  in absolute terms, so it is used only for **relative** capture.
- **Relative capture is stable:** across beta (1.3-1.95) and w (0-1), OTH would draw 0.65-0.86 times THI's intake and
  1.20-1.28 times TH Deggendorf's (OTH's pool is as large as Deggendorf's, Step 42, with fewer near competitors).
- OTH's existing informatics-type intake cannot be checked: the only public figure is engineering sciences as a
  whole (452 first-years in WS 2024/25).

## Result: first-years for an OTH Cyber Security B.Sc.

| Scenario | Basis | Year 1 | Year 2 | Year 3 = steady state |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Low | THI-anchored (THI intake = its capacity 25) x relative capture | 12 | 16 | **21** |
| Central | geometric mean of low and high (a choice) | 24 | 31 | **39** |
| High | Deggendorf-anchored (anecdotal ~60) x relative capture | 45 | 60 | **75** |

Ramp-up 60 % / 80 % / 100 % of steady state in the first three intake years is an assumption (awareness, first
graduates, school marketing). Ranges across the 9 model variants: low 16-21, high 72-77. A first intake should not be
planned for 2025 (G9 gap, Step 42); from 2026 the Bavarian Abitur cohort recovers (KMK Dok. 251: 5,995 in 2025,
29,240 in 2026), so 2026 or later is the better start.

## Minimum viable cohort

No Bavarian rule or guideline on a minimum first-year cohort was found (searched: BayHIG, ministry, admission statutes,
Wissenschaftsrat). **Assumption: 20 first-years per year** as the minimum for running a HAW bachelor; as a real-world
reference, THI runs its cyber bachelor with a planned capacity of 25.

| Scenario | Steady state | Year 1 | Meets 20 (steady) | Meets 20 in year 1 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Low | 21 | 12 | just | no |
| Central | 39 | 24 | yes | yes |
| High | 75 | 45 | yes | yes |

## Reading for the argument

- Claim B: an OTH cyber bachelor is **viable in the central and high scenarios and borderline in the low one**. The
  decisive uncertainty is not the model structure (relative capture is stable) but the real intake of comparable
  programmes: if THI's cyber bachelor really fills only ~25, OTH would reach ~20 and be at the edge.
- The best way to narrow the range is a direct question to THI and TH Deggendorf (or the state office) for their
  actual cyber first-year numbers; one figure would cut the range by about half.

## Caveats

- Only Bavarian counties are modelled; students from Saxony/Thuringia and from outside the region add to OTH's intake.
- The gravity calibration uses all subjects and Bezirk-level flows; cyber students may travel differently.
- The anchors are a planned capacity (THI) and an anecdote (Deggendorf), not measured intakes.
- Secondary competitors are identified from a ~400-character teaser (Step 38) and are incomplete.
