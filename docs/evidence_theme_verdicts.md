# Evidence: a written verdict for every theme group (candidate selection, data-sufficiency audit G5)

Table: `reports/theme_verdicts.md`, generated by `python -m study_program_intelligence.analysis.theme_verdicts`.

The original funnel (docs/evidence_funnel.md) gave verdicts for 9 theme groups. Three themes OTH already offers
(automation, energy, medtech/health) were dropped without a written reason. Five further themes in the code were never
screened (software engineering, sustainability, mobility, quantum, aerospace). Nor were the sub-themes data science,
health/medical informatics and energy informatics. This check applies the same two filters to all 18 themes, at
bachelor and master level.

## The two filters, written out

The thresholds were not written as numbers before. They are written here and checked against the original decisions.

1. **Demand trend.** Drop if the feeder subjects' first-years fell by more than 3 % a year in both Germany (2019-2025)
   and Bavaria (2019-2024). A small base (fewer than 500 feeder first-years in Germany 2025) goes to the watch list.
2. **Supply gap (per level).** Pass if there are at most 3 titled offers within 100 km of OTH and none at OTH.

German-only feeder trends are shown next to the filter values for information (docs/evidence_demand_split.md); the
filter uses all first-years, as the original funnel did.

## Agreement with the original funnel

For the original theme groups (20 theme-level rows; games/XR and media technology counted separately), the written
rules give the same outcome in 18 rows; 4 of them are drops by the other filter, and 2 rows differ.

|---|---|---|---|
| Energy / e-mobility (BA, MA) | dropped: already at OTH | dropped: feeders fall (-5.4 % Germany, -8.1 % Bavaria a year) | same outcome, other filter |

## New candidates by the written rules, and why none would reach the shortlist

These passed both filters but were not carried. The next stage (kill rule 1, EMI fit) was judged at title level only,
not tested:

| Theme | Level | Next stage (title-level judgement) |
|---|---|---|
| Embedded / IoT | BA | kill rule 1 likely: embedded systems are core content of OTH's E&IT and Industrie-4.0-Informatik bachelors; only 5 titled bachelors in Germany |
| Robotics | MA | kill rule 1 likely: robotics and ML for robotics are taught in OTH's KI programmes and the IT und Automation master |
| Software engineering | BA, MA | kill rule 1 likely: software engineering is the core of OTH's informatics bachelors; no Destatis subject of its own |
| Quantum | BA, MA | outside EMI's subjects (feeder = Physik, falling in Germany) |
| Aerospace | BA, MA | outside EMI's subjects (a mechanical-engineering field) |
| Health / medical informatics | BA, MA | small base: 600 first-years in Germany 2025, German first-years -5.3 % a year; overlap with OTH's Medizintechnik and Medical Engineering not tested; would score low on demand |
| Energy informatics | BA, MA | no titled programme in Germany: no market evidence |

Dropped by the filters: data science (crowded: 7 bachelor and 6 master offers within 100 km), sustainability (feeders
-11.2 % a year in Germany), mobility (at OTH or crowded), games/XR and media technology (feeders falling), and
microelectronics stays on the watch list (116 first-years in Germany 2025).

## Reading for the argument

- No theme outside the original shortlist is a stronger candidate than cybersecurity on the funnel's own filters.
  Cybersecurity's feeder base is the largest (25,005 first-years in Germany 2025).
- Health/medical informatics is the closest new case. It passes both filters, but its base is small and falling among
  German first-years. A full check (kill rule 1 against OTH's Medizintechnik and Medical Engineering) was not done; it
  is stated as a limitation.
- The written thresholds reproduce the original funnel in most rows. Where they differ, the original carried one level
  per theme; the rows it did not carry face a likely kill rule 1.

## Caveats

- The thresholds are written after the original screening, so they explain it rather than having been fixed before.
- Title keyword rules miss programmes whose titles do not name the theme; feeders are an assignment by us.
