# Kill rules

Written before any candidate is scored. Thresholds are agreed with the supervisor on [DATE].

A candidate is dropped if any rule below applies.

1. Overlap: most of its core modules already exist in one OTH programme, and no credible differentiator remains.
   Threshold: [TO AGREE, e.g. more than 50 % of core module titles or descriptions matched]

2. Feasibility: its feasibility rating is 1 (no staff, no lab, no accreditation path).

3. Trend: entrants in the related field fell in each of the last five years, and the KMK scenario shows no recovery.
   Threshold: [TO AGREE]

4. Scope: at most five candidates go into full dossiers. If more survive, rank them by the preliminary score and keep the top five.

## Scoring anchors (1 to 5)

To be written with observable thresholds before scoring. Example for student demand:

- 5: related-field entrants in Bavaria rose over five years, and the catchment supports a cohort at least as large as the one OTH enrols in a comparable programme.
- 1: related-field entrants fell over five years, and the catchment is too small for a cohort.

Anchors for the other criteria are added in week 1.
