# Kill rules

Written before any candidate is scored. Set by the team on 14 Oct 2026. Reviewed at checkpoint 1 with the supervisor.

A candidate is dropped if any rule applies. Rules 1 and 4 are applied per level: a bachelor candidate is compared with OTH's bachelor programmes, and a master candidate with OTH's master programmes.

1. Overlap: more than 50 % of its core modules already appear in one OTH programme at the same level, and no credible differentiator remains. Matching is done on module titles in published handbooks.

2. Feasibility: its feasibility rating is 1 (no staff, no lab, no accreditation path).

3. Trend: entrants in the related field across Germany fell in each of the last five years, and the KMK scenario shows no recovery.

4. Pipeline (master only): bachelor graduates in related fields across Germany are falling, and too few are in the region to fill a cohort without international applicants. Threshold to set in week 1 from the Destatis graduate data.

5. Scope: at most three candidates per level, six in total, go into full dossiers. If more survive, rank them by preliminary score and keep the top ones.

## Scoring anchors (1 to 5)

Written with observable thresholds before scoring. Example for student demand:

- 5: related-field entrants across Germany rose over five years, and the regional catchment supports a cohort at least as large as the one OTH enrols in a comparable programme.
- 1: related-field entrants across Germany fell over five years, and the regional catchment is too small for a cohort.

Anchors for the other criteria are added in week 1.
