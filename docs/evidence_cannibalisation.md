# Evidence: cannibalisation and regional benefit of an OTH Cyber Security B.Sc. (incrementality)

Status: v1, 17 Oct 2026 (Step 48). Tables: `reports/cannibalisation.md`; scenarios for Step 49 in
`data/interim/capture_scenarios.csv`, generated by `python -m study_program_intelligence.analysis.cannibalisation`.
Method decisions: docs/decision_log.md (Step 48). Public data only (no university intake figures).

## Method

The Step 47 Huff model (qualified school leavers by Bavarian county; distance decay calibrated on Bavarian student
flows, beta 1.3-1.95) is run without and with the new OTH programme. Competing alternatives: the four dedicated cyber
bachelors in Bavaria (THI, TH Deggendorf, THWS, HNU) and general bachelors with a security focus area (Step 38),
including OTH's own Industrie-4.0-Informatik and Medieninformatik, at weight w = 0.5 or 1. Under Huff, a new programme
draws from every alternative in proportion to its baseline share, which splits its students into:

- (a) **taken from OTH's existing programmes** (cannibalisation);
- (b) **won from other universities** (net new to OTH);
- (c) part of (b): students from OTH's own pool (Step 42) whose baseline choice lay **outside that region**:
  kept in the region (regional benefit).

## Results

Shares of the new programme's first-years, by model variant:

| beta | w | (a) from OTH | (b) other universities = net new | (c) kept in region |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1.30 | 0.5 | 14 % | 86 % | 15 % |
| 1.30 | 1.0 | 18 % | 82 % | 14 % |
| 1.60 | 0.5 | 18 % | 82 % | 16 % |
| 1.60 | 1.0 | 23 % | 78 % | 14 % |
| 1.95 | 0.5 | 22 % | 78 % | 16 % |
| 1.95 | 1.0 | 27 % | 73 % | 14 % |

Applied to the Step 47 range (low with the highest cannibalisation, high with the lowest):

| Scenario | Gross first-years (Step 47) | (a) from OTH programmes | (b) net new to OTH | (c) kept in region |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Low | 21 | 5.7 | **15.3** | 2.8 |
| Central | 39 | 7.0 | **32.0** | 6.1 |
| High | 75 | 10.6 | **64.4** | 11.5 |

**Stress test (limitation of the model):** the model only sees programmes with security in title or focus areas;
students who would otherwise take OTH's KI or E&IT bachelor without a security focus are not in (a). If true
cannibalisation were 50 % (about twice the model's maximum), net new first-years would be 10.5 / 19.5 / 37.5.

## Kill rule and incrementality

- **Threshold applied: kill rule 1 (docs/kill_rules.md, set 14 Oct 2026 before scoring): drop a candidate if more than
  50 % of its core modules already appear in one OTH programme at the same level.** kill_rules.md contains **no
  separate cannibalisation threshold**; we do not add one after scoring has started. Cannibalisation enters the
  ranking through the student-demand criterion, which per configs/analysis.yaml "includes the new-to-OTH share".
- Recheck with the Step 41/45 evidence: of the 15 security-specific modules in the draft bachelor (Step 45), 2 exist in
  one OTH bachelor (Industrie-4.0-Informatik: Informationssicherheit; Grundlagen der Codierungstheorie und Kryptologie)
  = 13 %; the module-level gap analysis gives at most 28 % of a typical cyber bachelor's security ECTS covered by
  Industrie-4.0-Informatik and Medieninformatik together (Step 41, upper-end estimate). Both are far below 50 %:
  **kill rule 1 is passed** (consistent with the 25 % of docs/overlap_test.md).

## Reading for the argument

- **Most students would be new to OTH**: 73-86 % (central 82 %) come from other universities, not from OTH's own
  programmes. Cannibalisation is limited (14-27 %) and concentrated on Industrie-4.0-Informatik and Medieninformatik,
  the two programmes with an information-security focus.
- **Regional benefit**: about one in seven students (13-16 %; central 6 per year) would come from OTH's own region and
  otherwise study in Ingolstadt, Deggendorf, Würzburg, Neu-Ulm or Munich.
- **Net new first-years: low 15, central 32, high 64.** The low case is below the assumed minimum cohort of 20
  (Step 47) even before the stress test; the central case is above it, also under the 50 % stress test (19.5 is
  borderline).

## For Step 49

`data/interim/capture_scenarios.csv` holds low / central / high for gross, (a), (b) and (c). Suggested Monte Carlo
input: net new first-years ~ triangular(15.3, 32.0, 64.4); gross ~ triangular(21, 39, 75).

## Caveats

- Fixed demand: the model does not create students who would not have studied at all, and it ignores students from
  outside Bavaria (both would raise net new).
- Cannibalisation outside security-focus programmes is not modelled (stress test above).
- Regional benefit counts only Bavarian counties in OTH's own pool; Regensburg is part of that pool, so students
  staying in Regensburg are not "kept in the region" by OTH.
