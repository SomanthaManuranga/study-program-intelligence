# Evidence: catchment of OTH Amberg-Weiden (Claim B: capture)

Status: v1, 17 Oct 2026 (Step 42). Tables: `reports/catchment.md`, generated by
`python -m study_program_intelligence.transform.school_leavers` and
`python -m study_program_intelligence.analysis.catchment run`. Method decisions: docs/decision_log.md (Step 42).

## Question

Does OTH have its own regional pool of potential first-year students, or does it share it with the competitors that
already teach cybersecurity (TH Deggendorf, which also has OT security; TH Ingolstadt; FAU/TH Nürnberg)?

## Data and method

- School leavers with a university entrance qualification by Bavarian county, school years 2013/14 to 2023/24
  (2018/19 missing: that edition is not in the archive): Bayerisches Landesamt für Statistik, Statistische Berichte
  B I/II 1 j "Bayerische Schulen - Eckzahlen ... nach kreisfreien Städten und Landkreisen", Tabelle 5 (public PDFs
  via the Statistische Bibliothek). Parsed with the table's own arithmetic; every year the 96 counties sum exactly to
  their Regierungsbezirk and the Bezirke to Bavaria.
- Three groups: **Gymnasium Abitur** (allgemeine Hochschulreife from general schools), **FOS/BOS** (Fachhochschulreife
  + Hochschulreife from vocational schools), and **all** (both, plus a small general-school Fachhochschulreife column
  until 2021/22).
- Counts are by school location, not residence. Sums over several neighbouring counties are robust; no city/Landkreis
  pair is split by the pool boundary.
- Distances: population-weighted county centres (Destatis municipality directory, 30.06.2026) to the OTH campuses and
  to the competitor locations of their cybersecurity programmes (Deggendorf; Ingolstadt; nearer of FAU Erlangen and
  TH Nürnberg). "Own pool" = counties closer to OTH than to all three. Sensitivity: OTH Regensburg as a fourth competitor.
- Forecast: 2023/24 values x the KMK projection for Bavaria (Dok. 251, same category).
- Not covered: 17 counties within 150 km in Saxony/Thuringia/Baden-Württemberg (2.5 m inhabitants). Their data is only
  in the Regionaldatenbank, whose robots.txt disallows all automated access; they mostly lie closer to universities in
  their own state.

## Reading

1. **Yes, OTH has its own regional pool, about 5,500 qualified school leavers a year (10 % of Bavaria).** It is almost
   exactly as large as TH Deggendorf's (5,600) and barely overlaps with it: 91 % of OTH's 100-km catchment is closer to
   Amberg/Weiden than to Deggendorf. So Deggendorf's OT-security offer competes with OTH mainly on profile, not on
   the same students.
2. **The real overlap is with Nuremberg/Erlangen**: only 42 % of OTH's 100-km catchment is closer to OTH than to the
   Nuremberg hub. But the only dedicated cyber programme there is FAU's part-time online bachelor (no full-time
   dedicated cyber bachelor at TH Nürnberg in the Step 38 list), so for a full-time cyber bachelor the nearest
   full-time competitors remain TH Ingolstadt (82 km) and TH Deggendorf (105 km).
3. **Regensburg is the swing area**: one third of the own pool (1,694) is in Regensburg city and county, 51 km from
   OTH and next to OTH Regensburg and Uni Regensburg (both with security focus areas, Step 38). Without it, the pool
   is 3,800 a year.
4. **The pool shrinks slowly and a little faster than Bavaria** (-19 % vs -13.5 % since 2013/14). The KMK projection
   implies a recovery to ~5,300 by 2030 and ~5,700 by 2035 after the 2025 G9 gap; with the region's own weaker trend
   continued, ~5,100-5,300. A new programme should not plan its first intake for autumn 2025 (G9 gap).
5. **FOS/BOS matters**: 40 % of the own pool comes via Fachoberschule/Berufsoberschule, the classic HAW route.

## Support for the argument

Claim B (capture): a distinct catchment of ~5,500 qualified leavers a year (~3,800 without Regensburg) with little
overlap with TH Deggendorf. Differentiation therefore rests less on the OT label and more on regional reach: a
full-time cyber bachelor in the northern Oberpfalz / eastern Oberfranken has no dedicated competitor nearer than 80 km.
Illustration (cohort size is an assumption, not yet derived): 40-60 first-years would be 0.7-1.1 % of the own pool
of 5,500 (1.1-1.6 % of the 3,800 without Regensburg), before counting students from further away.

## Caveats

- School location, not residence; students also move across county lines for school.
- Straight-line distances; real travel times (rail to Nuremberg/Regensburg) are not modelled.
- The nearest-site model ignores other universities (Bayreuth, Hof, Regensburg, Munich) and programme choice; it shows
  where OTH has a geographic advantage, not market shares.
- 2018/19 is missing; the 2019/20 drop is Bavaria-wide and not explained by this data (shares are unaffected).
- Forecast assumes the catchment follows the Bavarian KMK path (plus a sensitivity with the region's own trend).
- Saxony/Thuringia not included (data access); this understates the pool to the north-east.
