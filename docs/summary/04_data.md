# 4. Where the data came from

<!-- Plain English. Numbers only as {{key}} placeholders. -->

We used only public sources. Most of them come from public offices. Our core counts are official statistics, which
count everyone. A few sources are weaker signals, and we use them only for comparison or for our ranges.

| Source | What it tells us | Why we trust it |
|----------------------|------------------------------------|------------------------------|
| Federal Statistical Office (Destatis) | how many people start studying each subject, and how many graduate, 2005-2025 | official count of all students starting in the winter semester |
| Bavarian State Office for Statistics | school leavers per county; where the students of each Bavarian university of applied sciences come from; IT jobs in Bavaria | official counts |
| Forecasts of the education ministers (KMK) | how many school leavers and first-year students to expect until 2035 and beyond | official forecasts of the education ministers |
| Study search of the Federal Employment Agency | all {{funnel_offers}} bachelor and master offers in {{funnel_fields}} study fields | national catalogue; we checked our keyword search by hand ({{cyber_precision}} correct for cybersecurity) |
| Module handbooks | what each programme teaches, module by module | the universities' own official course plans |
| Job board and shortage report of the Federal Employment Agency | job adverts by keyword and region; jobs with too few applicants | official job board; the counts hardly changed between two days |
| Google Trends | how often people search for a degree subject | rough signal only, used to compare subjects |
| One student review | about {{deg_cyber_anecdote}} first-year students in cybersecurity at TH Deggendorf | weak; it sets our high estimate, and so also our middle one |

Three rules applied to all data. First, we respected each website's rules for automatic downloads. Second, where a site
blocked downloads or needed a login, we did not work around it. We used our own accounts where we had access, downloaded
public files by hand where only automatic downloads were blocked, or left the data out. Third, every statistic in this
summary comes from one generated list of **key numbers**, which names the document each number comes from.
