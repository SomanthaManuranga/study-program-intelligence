# 11. Glossary

<!-- Plain English. One plain sentence per term. Terms in bold are checked by the readability check. -->

**Anchor:** a known figure of a similar programme that turns a comparison into a number of students.

**Cannibalisation:** the part of a new programme's students who would otherwise have studied in the university's
existing programmes.

**Capture model:** a model of how many students each university attracts, based on how far from it students go to
school.

**Catchment:** the area closer to a university than to its competitors; for OTH, the Bavarian counties closer to it
than to the nearest universities with a cybersecurity programme.

**ECTS:** the European credit points for study work; a full bachelor at OTH has 210 ECTS.

**Key numbers:** the one generated list from which every statistic in this summary is taken, with the document it comes
from.

**Kill rule:** a rule, written in advance, that removes an option whatever its other strengths.

**Module handbook:** the official description of every course in a study programme.

**OT security:** the security of machines, factories and other technical systems, as opposed to office computers.

**Professorship:** a permanent professor's position, which comes with a fixed amount of teaching.

**Proxy:** something we can measure that moves together with something we cannot measure directly.

**Sensitivity check:** a test of whether the answer changes when one assumption is changed or left out.

**University of applied sciences:** a practice-oriented university such as OTH (Hochschule für angewandte Wissenschaften).

**Weights:** the importance given to each scoring point; they add up to one.

**What-if scenarios:** many repeats of the comparison, each with slightly different weights and scores, to see how often
each option wins.
