# 2. The question and why it matters

<!-- Plain English. Numbers only as {{key}} placeholders. -->

Our course gave us a clear task. Using only public data, we had to find which new study programme would attract the
most students to OTH, and explain why it suits our faculty.

This matters because, across Germany, most of the faculty's electrical and media subjects attract fewer new students
each year. Between 2019 and 2025 the number of first-year students changed by {{elektro_trend}} a year in electrical
engineering and by {{funnel_medientechnik}} a year in media technology. (2025 was a low year in Bavaria because of a
school reform.) Computer science is the exception. It grew over this period, although in Germany it has stayed about
level since 2023.

A new programme is a big decision. It needs new professors, rooms and equipment, and it takes years to show results. So
the faculty needs more than a good feeling. It needs to know whether enough students would come, whether they would be
new to OTH, whether the faculty can teach the subject, and whether graduates would find jobs.

We looked at bachelor's and master's programmes and at all of Germany for demand and competition. We used no internal
OTH data. All sources are public, and our project files record where each number comes from and when we took it.
