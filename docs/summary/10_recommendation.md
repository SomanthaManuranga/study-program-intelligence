# 10. What we recommend

<!-- Plain English. Numbers only as {{key}} placeholders. Proposals (a) and (b) are team judgement, labelled as such. -->

**Our recommendation: OTH should start a bachelor's degree in cybersecurity in the EMI faculty.** It was the only
finalist we tested in full on all four questions, and it passed each one. It also came first in
{{p_rank1_cyber_bsc_base}} of the what-if scenarios.

**What OTH would need:**

- **Students:** plan for about {{cyber_bsc_gross_central}} first-year students a year once the programme is fully
  running (range {{cyber_bsc_gross_low}} to {{cyber_bsc_gross_high}}), and fewer in the first two years.
- **Staff:** {{new_professorships}}, one for attack and forensics and one for defence and OT security, plus a lab
  engineer. Outside teachers would cover law and security management.
- **Room:** a separate security lab, ideally linked to the existing automation labs. We have not estimated its cost.
- **Profile:** a broad security bachelor with one module on factory security and a choice of electives. The name
  should use the English term "Cyber Security", because that is the term people use most when they search for a degree.
- **Start:** after accreditation. Bavaria's switch from eight to nine grammar-school years left one year with
  few school leavers (2025). That year is already past.

**Before deciding:** ask TH Ingolstadt and TH Deggendorf, or the Bavarian State Office for Statistics, for the real
number of first-year students in their cybersecurity bachelors. If the number is close to TH Ingolstadt's planned
{{thi_cyber_capacity}}, OTH should plan carefully and check the low case. If it is close to the figure for TH
Deggendorf, our middle estimate is on the careful side.

## Two proposals (team judgement, not model results)

**(a) The cybersecurity master later.** The master came second. We suggest offering it once the bachelor has its own
graduates. It could then share the bachelor's staff and lab and recruit its graduates.

**(b) Robotics inside the existing AI bachelor.** Robotics came last. {{robotics_overlap}} of its core topics are already
taught in OTH's AI bachelor. If half of its students came from OTH, only {{robotics_netnew_cannib50}} a year (low /
middle / high) would be new to OTH. These numbers rest on an assumed range of student numbers, because we
could not obtain a verified figure for a comparable programme. A stronger robotics focus within the AI bachelor would use the strong local robotics
job market without a new programme competing with an existing one.

**After a start:** compare the real number of first-year students, and the share from OTH's region, with our low,
middle and high cases every year.
