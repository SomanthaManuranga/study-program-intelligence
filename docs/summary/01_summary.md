OTH Amberg-Weiden should start a bachelor's degree in cybersecurity in its faculty of Electrical Engineering, Media and
Computer Science (EMI). We compared three possible new programmes using only public data. The cybersecurity bachelor came
first in {{p_rank1_cyber_bsc_base}} of {{mc_draws}} what-if scenarios. Our middle estimate is about
{{cyber_bsc_gross_central}} first-year students a year once the programme is fully running. In our model about four in
five of them would be new to OTH. The biggest uncertainty is how many students similar programmes really attract,
because none of the programmes we checked publishes this number.
